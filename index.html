The Big Pattern

Every major key works the same way

A major key contains seven basic chords. The chord qualities always follow the same pattern. Learn this pattern and every major key becomes easier to understand.

Major · minor · minor · Major · Major · minor · diminished
I · ii · iii · IV · V · vi · vii°

Uppercase Roman numerals mean major chords. Lowercase numerals mean minor chords. The small ° symbol means diminished.

Common guitar keys

Start with C, G, D, A, E, and F. They cover a huge amount of guitar music and keep the theory practical while you're learning.

  • Learn the I, IV, and V chords first.
  • Then add vi, the most commonly used minor chord in many pop and rock progressions.
  • Think in both chord names and Roman numerals.
